The AI Gallery Operating System

Five modules. One intelligent gallery.

  1. 01
    Step 01

    Artist Database

    Your artists, artworks, collectors and enquiries stay in the spreadsheets you already use.

    • Artist Profiles
    • Artworks & Inventory
    • Collector Records
  2. 02
    Step 02

    Smart Calendar

    Shows, studio visits, install days and deadlines are read from your calendar so nothing is missed.

    • Exhibitions
    • Deadlines & Reminders
    • Appointments
  3. 03
    Step 03

    Automation Engine

    n8n is the wiring. It spots a new row or date and passes the right information to the right place.

    • Change Detection
    • Smart Workflows
    • Auto Notifications
  4. 04
    Step 04

    AI Assistant

    A Qwen model running locally through Ollama reads what was gathered and writes the summary or email.

    • Summaries
    • Email Drafts
    • Reports & Insights
  5. 05
    Step 05

    Review & Send

    The finished text lands in Gmail as a draft. You read it, edit it, and press send yourself.

    • Review Drafts
    • Edit Content
    • Send with Confidence

Design principle

Your gallery data stays under your control.

The AI model runs locally through Ollama. The included workflows are designed for human review, use environment-based configuration, and keep credentials out of the repository.
